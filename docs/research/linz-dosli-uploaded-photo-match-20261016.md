## Reuse and technical status

The files are readable as displayed images, but raw-byte materialisation was denied by the available Library interface. This commit **does not** claim to add image bytes to GitHub, image collection metadata, or a deployed gallery. The existing site image reference `/images/landlink-1999-topographic-database/` explicitly states that the local copy is unavailable. Transfer the authorised originals into the repository with credit and provenance before showing them publicly. Do not identify the anonymous people in the historic photographs by appearance. The 1999 clipping's *own printed caption* is the source of named staff associations.

The 1999 newsletter should accompany the LINZ institution record and digital topographic database event; DOSLI staff and equipment photos are more appropriate to the predecessor's organisation card and earlier topographic conversion chapters. The unrelated anonymous portrait should remain in the research collection until its subject and date are documented.
