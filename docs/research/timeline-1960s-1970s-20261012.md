Related chapter links are contextual and do not imply these events were already digitally managed.

## Deferred date discrepancy

The Manaaki Whenua narrative describes publication of the general South Island soil survey as 1969. Its Soil Bureau Bulletin register lists *General survey of the soils of South Island, New Zealand* (Bulletin 27) as **1968**. This should be resolved against the original bulletin title pages, associated maps and cataloguing records before adding a dated event. Do not repeat the uncertain 1969 date as established.

## Image discipline

The chapter navigation uses the same paragraph-reviewed documentary images or source-linked embedded figures as the story. It does not assign archive-only items to unrelated chapters. Pages without a reviewed documentary image keep a text-based navigation button. Chapter images retain their original colours, descriptive alternative text and a link to their image details.
