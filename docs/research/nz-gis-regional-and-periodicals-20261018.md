## Candidates

The 19 original source URLs include seven DOC conservation and geospatial science reports (2004–2022), eight Ministry for the Environment planning, land-use, freshwater and geospatial publications (2016–2022), three Greater Wellington landscape, Hutt and Kāpiti GIS publications (2009, 2012 and 2024), and one Bay of Plenty Regional Council annual report documenting operational GIS (2015).

DOC and MfE default Crown material is generally CC BY 4.0 subject to any document-specific third-party restrictions. Direct publishers' published copyright statements: https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/ and https://environment.govt.nz/about-this-site/copyright/. The archive script rejects detected document-specific prohibitions and requires document-level grants for council PDFs. Ordinary web availability alone is not treated as a republication grant.

## Further periodical discoveries

Searches located early use of geographic information systems in NIWA's *Water & Atmosphere* (2002–2008), including GIS applications in fisheries and sediment databases, as well as *NZ Search and Rescue Link* (2010, 2012, 2022, 2024) and *GeoConnexion* (2015). These are useful contextual evidence, but complete original issues are not automatically republished where an open-copy licence is not established. Their article references remain in `docs/research/nz-gis-periodicals-source-audit-20261018.md`.

National Library catalogues document *Surveying + Spatial* issues 79–119 from 2014 to autumn 2025 and the *New Zealand Surveyor* archive. The existing contributor-owned GIS upskilling article from 2021 must not be duplicated.

Waikato Regional Council records discovered for modelling whitebait habitat (2014), wetland remote sensing (2016), soil stability (2016), surf-break GIS (2017) and spatial planning tools (2023) require source-specific reuse verification. Publication of unlicensed complete PDFs is excluded; the historical events and methods may be researched and cited independently.

## Integration and checking

Index accepted sources by original publication year, produce genuine first-page JPEG covers, ensure `/publications/pdf/<slug>/` displays the original without forced download, and prevent missing or external-only PDFs from appearing as in-site reading pages. Include article- or chapter-level additions only where claims can be supported by the original publication, and preserve existing passage checksums through the project manifest workflow.
