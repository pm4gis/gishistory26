# Google Earth and Street View in New Zealand, 13 October 2026

## Coverage

Chapter 31 already describes Google Earth's international 28 June 2005 release, Google Maps' December 2007 New Zealand launch, the 2007 Tourism New Zealand layer, the 2006 Maps API geocoding support, and KML distribution. This review adds documented New Zealand experiences without duplicating those accounts.

- 2 December 2008: Street View launches here as the seventh country, initially including both urban and rural roads. Source: https://australia.googleblog.com/2008/12/street-view-now-in-new-zealand.html
- 21 October 2009: Google announces a second driving programme to refresh existing street-level imagery and improve image quality and face blurring. Source: https://newzealand.googleblog.com/2009/10/street-view-in-new-zealand-to-get.html. Consider a later timeline entry only if the re-drive deserves a separate technical milestone.
- 14 December 2010: Privacy Commissioner reports unfair Wi-Fi information collection and unlawful communications payload capture during Street View filming. Source: https://www.privacy.org.nz/resources-and-learning/public-inquiries/googles-collection-of-wifi-information-during-street-view-filming/. Keep image-taking, openly broadcast Wi-Fi identifiers and unsecured network payload distinct.
- 2011: DPMC's archive includes a Christchurch central-city cordon KML file and a Google Earth version with 22 February 2011 dataset date. Source: https://www.dpmc.govt.nz/publications/other-cera-publications-and-data. This is an archive record, not proof of a same-day release by CERA.
- 5 November 2012: Google Earth Outreach announced for Australia and New Zealand. Source: https://maps.googleblog.com/2012/11/google-earth-outreach-launches-in.html. No named New Zealand beneficiary established from this announcement alone.
- 23 April 2014: Google's historical Street View feature documents Christchurch streets before and after the earthquakes. Source: https://blog.google/intl/en-nz/products/2014_04_preserving-christchurchs-history-to/. The official source contains relevant before/after imagery requiring separate reuse checks.
- June 2019: LEARNZ's Map My Wāhi field trip centred on Lower Hutt. John Bailey explained Google Earth, Voyager and Tour Builder; other videos documented families and civic heritage at Belmont School; Duane Wilkins demonstrated Esri ArcGIS StoryMaps. Sources: https://www.learnz.org.nz/mapping192/videos and https://www.linz.govt.nz/our-work/location-information/geospatial-capability/geospatial-schools/map-my-wahi-discovering-our-shared-heritage. LINZ currently notes Tour Builder has been retired.

## Distinctions and limits

Street View launched within Google Maps, while Google Earth was the 3D globe and KML viewer; later versions integrated Street View. Google Earth satellite and aerial views, the Street View panorama archive, the 2019 LEARNZ videos, and ArcGIS StoryMaps have different creators, dates and roles. Use authentic screenshots or original video frames only once the precise item and its reuse conditions have been reviewed.

The original user description ‘Lower Hutt video’ does not identify a single title. The 2019 LEARNZ series is a documented candidate, but this review has not proved it is the exact video intended.
