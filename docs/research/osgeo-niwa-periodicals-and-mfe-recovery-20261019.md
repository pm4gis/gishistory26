## Separate magazines with reproduction limitations

NIWA Water & Atmosphere 2002–2008 contains first-hand GIS work on Lake Taupō fish habitat, national sediment-yield modelling, stream network connectivity, freshwater systems, RiskScape and marine GIS; source and issue detail in `docs/research/nz-gis-magazines-original-sources-20261018.md`. Some original magazines request express editorial permission for reprinting. Their primary historical findings can inform referenced chapter text but their complete binaries remain out of the locally hosted collection until a specific grant is established.

Surveying + Spatial, New Zealand Surveyor and NZ Local Government Magazine serials likewise remain historical source leads rather than unlicensed complete original PDF acquisitions. Reassess per issue, not by broad inference about all magazines.

## Ministry for the Environment technical PDF recovery

MfE's `environment.govt.nz/assets/` source URLs were publicly indexed in October 2026, but attempts from GitHub Actions with Node fetch and curl encountered redirect loops of more than 50 hops. Versioned query URLs also failed. Official publication landing pages remain available for the 2017 Plan Mapping Standards discussion paper F (ME1299) and the 2018 Electronic Functionality and Accessibility and Mapping evaluation (ME1360): https://environment.govt.nz/publications/plan-mapping-standards/ and https://environment.govt.nz/publications/proposed-nps-evaluation-report-2018-part-2e-electronic-functionality-and-accessibility-and-mapping/. Both describe historical ePlans, online plan viewers, spatial layers and GIS mapping standards; neither has yet been acquired as an eligible original PDF.

A source recovery should try a demonstrably original binary from an authorised alternate official repository, publisher archive, legacy m.f.e. address or historical signed URL, verify year/ISBN/file checksum and preserve the original in the site. Do not substitute a newly generated PDF of extracted text or a separately typeset copy and call it the original. No public-facing external-only document links or archive-policy commentary.

## Second DOC historical periodical tranche

The archived first batch (PR #124) contains 15 GIS-relevant original DOC periodical PDFs from 1993–2004. Source excerpts are in `docs/verification/doc-science-newsletter-gis-evidence-20261018.json`. The second batch screens further Rare Bits and Conservation Science Newsletter issues for actual GIS, GPS, Biosite, remote-sensing or spatial-database references before adding them to the live library. Existing original bytes and historical bibliographic dates are retained. Each new issue receives a SHA-256 checksum, authentic first-page image and local reader URL. Do not double-count previously archived originals.
