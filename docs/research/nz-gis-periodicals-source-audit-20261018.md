# NZ GIS periodicals and council sources: focused acquisition pass

Research date: 18 October 2026. Historical cutoff: 31 December 2025. Internal research notes.

## Candidates for local PDF preservation

- DOC, Conservation Science Newsletter, issues 28–29 (18 December 1998), page 16. A proposed GIS database of fire-dependent and fire-vulnerable habitats. https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/science-and-technical/conservation-science-newsletter/conservation-science-newsletter-28--29-december-1998.pdf
- Geospatial Industry Profile Survey Committee (2023). Baseline geospatial workforce survey with 523 respondents. https://www.linz.govt.nz/our-work/location-information/geospatial-capability/working-new-zealands-geospatial-industry/new-zealand-geospatial-industry-profile-survey
- Auckland Council, TR2021/19, Auckland Liquefaction Assessment. Contains GeoMaps methodology and CC BY 4.0. https://knowledgeauckland.org.nz/media/hcgdrqni/tr2021-19-auckland-liquefaction-assessment-and-liquefaction-vulnerability-maps.pdf
- Auckland Council, TR2016/002, Watercourse assessment methodology (version 2.0). Includes ArcGIS Online, geodatabases and field mapping. The publication permits accurate, attributed non-commercial educational/public-service reproduction. https://www.knowledgeauckland.org.nz/media/1267/tr2016-002-watercourse-assessment-methodology-infrastructure-and-ecology-v2.pdf
- Auckland Council (December 2025), Water quality and river ecology data explorer user guide and methodology. Original states CC BY 4.0. https://knowledgeauckland.org.nz/media/gy3jf2fq/water-quality-river-ecology-data-explorer-user-guide-methodology-supplementary-report-eemu-dec-2025.pdf
