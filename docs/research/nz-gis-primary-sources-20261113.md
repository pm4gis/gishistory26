## Rechecks and editorial decisions

- [Pascoe 1988](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/35094855-0c2e-4ac0-baf2-cf07bb2fd513): Canterbury lists `pascoe_report.pdf` and explicitly credits Richard T. Pascoe with copyright. Its abstract anachronistically refers to LINZ in 1988; do not repeat this institutional attribution without inspecting the original title page.
- [Perry 1996](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/aeb7aebd-e9e2-4f09-a433-3fbbed1dfe69): thesis and three original map PDFs listed; already V04 in the 26 October register, so no duplicate record.
- [Kliskey 1993](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/doctoral/Wilderness-perception-mapping--a-geographic/9926479054501891): repository dates it 11 December 1993, but file is named `KliskeyAndrewD1992PhD.pdf`; original title page remains necessary to settle date.
- [McLennan 1998](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/graduate/Spatial-information-systems-for-wildlife-conservation/9926478546601891): Otago lists a 9.28 MB PDF and substantive original SIS prototype. Distinct from 1996 conference paper V09.
- [MacDonell and Benwell 1996](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/workingPaper/Process-management-for-geographical-information-system/9926480439301891), [Lilburne et al. 1996](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/workingPaper/GIS-expert-systems-and-interoperability/9926479884101891), and [Holt et al. 1998](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/workingPaper/Spatial-isomorphism/9926480469001891): original GIS-specific working papers with PDFs listed, but Otago copyright restrictions prevent treating open-access downloads as permission to mirror.
- [England 2011](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/61976ffe-cfe4-43e2-8e72-f0e3d767c8f5): substantive GIS landslide inventory and susceptibility mapping of the West Coast; 15.71 MB thesis PDF listed, copyright reserved. Lower priority than pre-2000 original sources and external-only.
- [de Gouw 2018](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/298920fc-0625-4be6-a693-42b105a16b14): direct evidence on NZ forestry-sector adoption of GIS, GPS and remote sensing, PDF listed, copyright reserved. Lower priority and external-only.
- [Kirkpatrick 1991](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/3486bb82-0e98-4fd0-af53-62d9e02d5726): cartographic communication and user-driven mapping. GIS-specific relevance is not established by the abstract, so exclude from GIS PDF catalogue unless the full text supplies substantive evidence.
- [Brewster 1994](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/report/Planning-a-safe-city-for-women/9926479548201891): urban safety and planning, without substantive GIS evidence in abstract; reject.
- [Mason 1992](https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/b9883923-8b9f-46b4-831b-6e660deaabe0): forestry decision-support modelling, but no demonstrated GIS component in abstract; hold outside catalogue.
