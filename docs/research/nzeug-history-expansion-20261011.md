# New Zealand Esri Users Group research expansion
11 October 2026. Base commit: `c8a8bbf235e38c93f7bbd7b4a2650f3fba401861`. Proposed branch: `research/nzeug-history-expansion-20261011`.

## Confirmed evidence
- NZEUG's own organisational history gives a foundation year of 1992. A 2026 committee profile records Rachel Summers recalling what she called the first meeting in Dunedin in 1994. These are recorded as distinct accounts, not reconciled as a single disputed date. The original foundation minutes were not found. [NZEUG profile](https://nz.linkedin.com/company/new-zealand-esri-users-group)
- The annual conference sequence begins in 1996. 2005 is documented as the tenth and 31 October 2006 as the eleventh. NZEUG's history omits 2007, while 2012 is described as the sixteenth annual conference. **There is no basis to label 2007 cancelled.** [Chapter 29 chronology](/stories/becoming-a-gis-professional/#user-groups-become-infrastructure-18b0a31a28)
- The 2012 Asia Pacific User Conference in Auckland ran 5–7 November at SKYCITY, organised by Eagle Technology and incorporating NZEUG's sixteenth annual event. Esri reported **530 attendees**, **20 countries**, **more than 220 organisations**. A separate contemporaneous advance report says NZEUG represented **more than 300 member organisations**. These are separate statistics and must not be combined. [Esri 2012 recap](https://www.esri.com/about/newsroom/arcnews/esri-regional-user-conferences-spread-arcgis-know-how-around-the-globe) and [Esri advance conference report](https://www.esri.com/news/arcnews/fall12articles/files/arcnews34-3/arcnews-fall12.pdf)
- Esri names Kate Burns, James Lyth and Kate Williams of Tonkin & Taylor as the 2012 Excellence in GIS award recipients for Canterbury Earthquake Residential Land Damage. Afaf Abbas of Opus International Consultants and Manas Chakraborty of Horizons Regional Council received honourable mentions for transport and freshwater work respectively. Ryan Clements of Queenstown Lakes District Council won the WebMap Award. Esri also recorded DOC's Norm Thornley commenting on the programme. [Esri retrospective](https://www.esri.com/about/newsroom/arcnews/esri-regional-user-conferences-spread-arcgis-know-how-around-the-globe)
- Eagle's published 20–22 August 2018 agenda records DOC, LINZ, local government, industry and university presentations. Its 21 August programme names **Roland Pomana** as the NZEUG AGM facilitator, **Anna Eilers** as local/regional government session facilitator, **Matthew Grose** as a DOC presenter and **Nathan Heazlewood** as a land-management and project-management facilitator. [2018 programme](https://www.eagle.co.nz/sites/default/files/NZEUC_2018_Final.pdf)
- 2024 organiser-reported regional attendance totals, already accepted in chapter 29, are approximately **650 people from 400 organisations at eight events**. NZEUG's 2025 posts document Auckland and Christchurch regional meetings with local practitioners, students and named sponsors. [Auckland 2025 report](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/new-zealand-esri-users-group_auckland-nzeug-networking-activity-7315305187932352512-ii9u) and [Christchurch 2025 report](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/new-zealand-esri-users-group_nzeug25-christchurch-nzeug25-activity-7315660920276328449-jvS9).
- The user group maintains a [2017–2025 AGM minutes collection](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/collections/3815273d952741d893b27e01de989fcb). Individual linked AGM documents were not extracted for this batch; they remain a useful follow-up source, not evidence for invented office-holder dates.
