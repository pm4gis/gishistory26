# Property platforms and LINZ data

Base revision: `492b728bf8656b0daed988fe0fea57901363f7af` on `main`, retrieved 18 October 2026.

Homes.co.nz and OneRoof are included as organisation records with their public websites and source-backed relationships to Land Information New Zealand. The records describe each platform as combining LINZ data with other information, rather than implying LINZ is its only source.

The New Zealand Government's data.govt.nz showcase says Homes.co.nz combines property data obtained from local councils with open data, including addresses and property boundaries from the LINZ Data Service and school-enrolment-zone data from the Ministry of Education. Homes.co.nz's own HomesEstimate FAQs also describe council records as a source for property details and valuation inputs.

OneRoof's disclaimer identifies LINZ as the source for title number, title type, legal description and estate description. It separately identifies local authorities as sources for other property attributes, rating valuations and sale history. OneRoof's real-estate page describes its consumer platform and audience.

No historical event or chapter date is added. The entries document public-facing uses of geospatial and property information, with the evidence and source URLs retained on the records and relationship edges.
