# Ngā Poutama Matawhenua audiovisual catalogue and contextual integration

**Publication work:** 11 October 2026 (Aotearoa local date). **Repository:** `pm4gis/gishistory26`. **Base:** merged PR #26. **Scope:** genuine public recordings, not the republication of YouTube assets or permission to reproduce iwi-held cultural data.

## Evidence and completeness

LINZ's official [Ngā Poutama Matawhenua playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLyE56PxGCqSXGUtFIvpNn0b_SauF4XfIk) is the primary discovery source. The matching public [YouTube playlist Atom feed](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?playlist_id=PLyE56PxGCqSXGUtFIvpNn0b_SauF4XfIk) exposed **13 distinct entries** on 11 October 2026. These include original publisher descriptions and UTC publication dates; descriptions are not independently verified transcripts. Two additional already identified publicly linked recordings, `yCBeRHA2Jnk` (2021 conversation) and `KYEVxcBEVQ8` (sites of significance, upload date unknown), were retained and checked against their own video pages and the existing MāoriGIS.nz resource index. **15 distinct recordings are indexed in the release.**

The playlist HTML page does not expose its complete historical membership through ordinary extraction and the Atom feed is a recent-item window rather than a historical export. A full playlist count and enumeration of earlier sessions therefore remain **unverified**. Do not call these 15 the complete archive. The site explicitly links to the official live playlist for older and new material. A browser-driven attempt to enumerate the rest was blocked by a third-party browser service credit limit; no unverifiable older items were invented.

Upload dates below come from the feed, apart from the separately verified 2021 conversation. **Upload dates are not necessarily session dates**: for example, the Keith Holswich wānanga took place on 13 June 2025 and was published on 2 July 2025, independently corroborated by [LINZ's July 2025 Te Pokapū newsletter](https://www.linz.govt.nz/news/2025-07/te-pokapu-july-2025).

Descriptions establish the roles of **Duane Wilkins** as presenter for the historical aerial imagery and AI records, **Jonathan Hanson** for the GeoNet earth-science presentation, **Georgia Edwards** for BayMaps, **Jordon Traill** for SIT, **Holden Hohaia** for the QGIS presentation, **Tania Te Hira** in a Census-data question-support role, and **Keith Holswich** for He Kete Mātauranga. Duane Wilkins is separately a facilitator for the *series*; that does not imply he presented every session. Where the feed description does not identify an individual presenter, the catalogue says so.

## Published integration design

- `src/lib/nga-poutama-videos.mjs`: source-backed video IDs, titles, upload dates, topic tags, descriptions, speaker credits, per-passage IDs, relevant existing events and presenter/entity links.
- `src/components/WanangaVideos.svelte`: full 15-video catalogue with contextual kaupapa filters on the Māori GIS theme and Ngā Poutama organisation page. It provides linked YouTube thumbnails and direct watch URLs, not copyrighted downloads.
- `src/components/Reader.svelte`: inserts only applicable video cards **between the paragraphs** of relevant passages, both in complete-chapter and page-by-page mode. The previous duplicated end-of-chapter gallery has been removed, preventing 15 records being dumped onto unrelated chapters. The in-passage cards occupy a labelled horizontal scroller (with keyboard focus and reduced-motion support), so seven relevant sessions cannot produce several screens of uninterrupted video cards on a phone.
- `src/components/Timeline.svelte`: the selected event displays a direct source-video link where the corresponding public milestone is supported by the catalogue. The historical date, summary and canonical event source remain unchanged.
- Direct links to relevant **existing** people/organisation profiles are added only where the publisher identifies the participant. These links never assign an image to a Person profile.

For correct historical interpretation, 2026 recordings are explicitly labelled as later supplementary demonstrations; their contents cannot be treated as evidence that the same tools or project practices existed in the 2025 historical period. The original descriptions are paraphrased, not copied wholesale.
