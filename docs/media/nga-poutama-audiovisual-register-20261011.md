# Ngā Poutama Matawhenua recorded-source register — 11 October 2026

Repository `pm4gis/gishistory26`. Continues the earlier four-record gallery published in PR #26. The data source of truth is `src/lib/nga-poutama-videos.mjs`; that file also contains the curated topic, source link, textual description, named presenters where evidenced, exact historical passage ID, and associated existing record IDs.

## Evidence and completeness

Fourteen recordings were extracted from the public **Toitū Te Whenua / LINZ YouTube playlist Atom feed**, `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?playlist_id=PLyE56PxGCqSXGUtFIvpNn0b_SauF4XfIk`. Feed video IDs, title and publication date (UTC) were taken from that public source on 11 October 2026; presenter details are recorded only where the published descriptions name them. The recorded date of a wānanga and the publication date of its edited recording are explicitly distinguished.

Two additional previously verified public recordings appear outside the accessible 14-entry feed window: the 2021 conversation with Duane Wilkins, Roland Pomana and Matt Grose, and a sites-of-significance demonstration identified in the MāoriGIS.nz public resource index. Its individual presenter and date are **unconfirmed**.

**This is a 16-record verified selection, not a complete historical listing of every video in the LINZ playlist.** The playlist web interface requires scrolling to enumerate older recordings; full scrolling could not be completed because the available browser automation wallet was exhausted. Do not fill the gap with guessed IDs or infer presentation authorship from video thumbnails. The playlist remains linked as the authoritative route to older/other records.

## Integration

- `/nga-poutama-videos/` is the new searchable audiovisual index, with topical filtering and direct links to the corresponding existing historical passage.
- `/themes/maori-gis/` and `/entities/nga-poutama-matawhenua/` continue to feature four verified recordings, and now link to the complete sixteen-record documented index.
- `src/components/Reader.svelte` now inserts each video *between the original blocks of its relevant historical passage*, both in complete-chapter mode and page-by-page reading. No chapter paragraph, original block, passage ID, people profile or existing image was changed.
- Where a 2024–26 recording accompanies discussion of a much older event, its caption explicitly calls it a **later demonstration**, not original footage of the historical event.
- All external video thumbnails remain hotlinked from `i.ytimg.com`; no copies of YouTube media, screengrabs, music or recordings are published in the repository or the historical image catalogue.
- The presenter credit is independent of Duane Wilkins's LINZ-documented series facilitation. For example, Keith Holswich is specifically credited for He Kete Mātauranga, Jonathan Hanson for the earthquake session, Georgia Edwards for BayMaps, Karl Majorhazi and Tania Te Hira for Kōrero Takutai, and Holden Hohaia and Tania Te Hira for census/environmental mapping.
- Where an individual has no existing Person record or an independent historical biography, named credit in the audiovisual record does not automatically imply a newly published biography.
