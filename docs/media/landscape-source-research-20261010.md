# Landscape imagery provenance research
10 October 2026. Research companion to `landscape-selection-review.md`. Status: **all five images pending, no public image additions**.

## Verification standard
The initial 162-photo catalogue comprises manually prepared PNG wallpapers, not georeferenced source orthophotos. For individual screenshots, the original LINZ imagery layer, collection, capture timeframe and actual copyright/licensor must be traced before assigning an approval. The printed text “Source: data.linz.govt.nz” or “Imagery via Land Information New Zealand” confirms a stated source platform, not which imagery licence applies. LINZ says it does **not** own all imagery copyright and gives a template requiring the layer URL and named licensor: https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/licensing-and-using-data/attributing-elevation-or-aerial-imagery-data . The Basemaps guidance also warns that aerial backgrounds can combine LINZ open imagery with satellite sources owned by other parties: https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/licensing-and-using-data/attributing-linz-basemaps-data .

All date evidence below comes from Drive file metadata, not from a photograph's capture metadata. An image stored in 2020 cannot depict an imagery collection first flown in 2022 or 2025. Conversely, a dataset publication date later than a screenshot does not by itself rule out a precursor publication of the same earlier photographic survey in a different service; use this distinction in research.

## Authoritative research entrypoints

- LINZ [NZ Imagery Survey Index](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/95677-nz-imagery-survey-index/) gives survey footprints and survey metadata across datasets. See the [LINZ imagery-surveys data dictionary](https://nz-imagery-surveys.readthedocs.io/en/latest/published_data.html). The coordinates within these wallpapers are point locations, not enough to establish the exact survey where footprints overlap.
- LINZ [public aerial imagery STAC registry](https://github.com/linz/imagery), including original collection metadata; imagery capture dates can differ from collection title year ranges.
- LINZ [access aerial imagery](https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/types-linz-data/aerial-imagery/access-aerial-imagery), which confirms survey/tile metadata availability.
- LINZ [Northland regional imagery catalogue](https://data.linz.govt.nz/data/?geotag=global%2Foceania%2Fnew-zealand%2Fnorthland), [Porirua catalogue](https://data.linz.govt.nz/data/?geotag=global%2Foceania%2Fnew-zealand%2Fwellington%2Fporirua-city), [Otago/Dunedin catalogue](https://data.linz.govt.nz/data/?geotag=global%2Foceania%2Fnew-zealand%2Fotago%2Fdunedin-city).
- [Tasman 0.3m Rural Aerial Photos (2022)](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/109466-tasman-03m-rural-aerial-photos-2022/) and [Tasman 0.25m Rural Aerial Photos (2024–2025)](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/122529-tasman-025m-rural-aerial-photos-2024-2025/) serve as specifically *excluded* later datasets, rather than claimed sources.

## Assessment

No original has an embedded georeferenced imagery collection ID or source licence. The five candidate images have independent file timestamps and checksums in the supplied processing package, but those do not prove who licenced the underlying photography. The screenshot title, modified date and the overlay help narrow research. Without the original layer record, an auditor could not confirm the licensor. Consequently **zero images have passed the publication gate**.

The PR contains a dormant component, verification code, selection guidance and a dry-run installer. No binary assets have been added to public paths. The processing package is retained separately. Do not use the shorter “Sourced from LINZ. CC BY 4.0” wording to claim a specific unknown image is CC BY without first determining its provenance. When approved, show the correct licensor, service, original layer link and licence with the photograph.

Next action is to cross-check exact locations against candidate orthophoto tile indexes and STAC collection assets from relevant capture years, then compare image features before approving any derivative. Do not copy newer raster metadata onto older wallpapers.
