# Homepage chapter thumbnail editorial register

13 October 2026

## Scope

All 44 homepage chapter cards should have real, relevant images. The existing 19 image placements verified against chapter passages are preserved. The 25 new choices below apply **only to homepage card thumbnails**, and cannot alter the chapters' text, paragraph illustrations, historical claims, reader's previous/next chapter images, or the separately maintained image archive. Every selected asset is already present as a documentary image in `src/content/images/` and linked to a source and credit on its own detail page.

The image's original date, publisher, subject and source licence remain as recorded in the existing image metadata. Some chapter-level illustrations are later examples of a technique; none is claimed to show a particular early system unless the source actually identifies it. Cartographic outlines and labels are never redrawn or invented.

## Presentation and quality checks

- Photographic images may use centre-cropping inside a thumbnail frame, without recolouring or changing their actual geographic subject. Maps, survey plans and interface screenshots retain full-frame `object-fit:contain` to avoid hiding contours, labels, legends or application controls.
- Map and photograph alt text is taken directly from the authenticated original image record. Card images link to the relevant chapter. Original credits and licences remain on the image's own page.
- The `chapterNavImageMap` and `placeChapterImages` editorial logic for in-story/reader content remains unchanged. The homepage selection register is explicit and independently testable.
- `tests/home-chapter-thumbnail-coverage.test.mjs` checks that all 44 source-backed image records resolve, the 19 established choices remain the same, and the new choices cannot silently become paragraph-level evidence.
- The homepage Chromium browser tests validate all 44 rendered thumbnails, source image loading in a representative sample, accessible alt text and scroll/overflow checks across desktop and mobile.
- No generic photographs, decorative landscapes, fake maps or image-generation assets are introduced in place of documentary originals.

**Caution:** The KiwiRail image is a passenger-rail network map, included solely to illustrate a geographic network as a related concept. It is not a map of an electricity, telecommunications or water network. The NASA World Wind screen is a 2006 contemporary digital-globe comparator and is explicitly different from Google Earth. These distinctions should remain visible in editorial records and source alt text.
