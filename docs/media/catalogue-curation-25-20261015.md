# Catalogue imagery curation: 25 entries

Prepared 15 October 2026 from production baseline `856cd2a` in `pm4gis/gishistory26`.

## Method and boundaries

These 25 listings previously displayed decorative landscape fallbacks. Their replacements reuse **existing archived original images and derivatives** from `src/content/images` and `public/assets/historical` rather than importing new uncontrolled image files. Each original archive entry provides a source URL, credit, recorded reuse terms and local image assets. Rights information below reproduces the recorded terms and should be checked against the linked source before republishing an original outside this site. The rights metadata is not an independent legal opinion.

An image that genuinely records the named dataset, agency output or historic map series is distinguished from a predecessor, a related source or a contextual photograph. Contextual images must never be labelled as a product of the named agency. Full relationship notes and source links are shown on the record's content page. The catalogue image itself always links to the content entry; the separate image information page retains its original source and licence information.

## Validation and residual gaps

The machine-readable list in `docs/media/catalogue-curation-25-20261015.json` supplies the exact 25 record and image slugs and their relationship classes. `tests/catalogue-curation-25.test.mjs` verifies records, archived source URLs, credits, reuse terms, alt text, build assets and agreement between all catalogue views. The Chromium catalogue test checks the new images and click destinations across desktop and mobile.

These 25 entries now have **documentary illustrations**, but that is distinct from finding an original GIS map produced by every named organisation. The current archive still lacks verified, licensed direct GIS outputs for Geographx, the New Zealand Forest Service, MPI, CERA, Hawke’s Bay Regional Council, Otago Regional Council, Kāpiti Coast District Council and several further contextual selections. Their published captions are explicit about these limits. Do not replace the contextual examples with unsourced logos, speculative screenshots or generated historic maps.

The historical prose, chapter images, historical dates, records, IDs and relationship edges have not been changed by this curation. Existing subject-image credit and licence pages continue to link from the associated content pages.
