# Direct-source GIS imagery follow-up

Research and publication decisions, 15 October 2026. Project: `pm4gis/gishistory26`.

## Scope

This is a dated follow-up to `catalogue-curation-25-20261015.md`, rather than a rewrite of that accepted 25-entry curation snapshot. Eight catalogue entries were investigated for original institutional maps, geospatial system screenshots or historical GIS products. The archive and existing media relationships were inspected before external research. Source dates and reuse conditions were checked wherever practical. No chapter prose, historical records or timeline events were changed.

Image licence evidence is assessed at the **specific image** level. A CC BY licence on geographic input data, another illustration from the same organisation, or an underlying base map does not automatically license a completed third-party screenshot or cartographic publication. A record that needs clearer rights, provenance or source-image retrieval stays unchanged.

## Technical controls and quality checks

The newly published CERA image is recorded as `cera-central-recovery-blueprint-2012` in `src/content/images/`, with preserved source JPEG, web and card derivatives, original and display SHA-256, original dimensions, full credit, licence, source URL and an explicit non-chapter publication decision in `docs/media/chapter-image-alignment-wave2-20261011.csv`. The image binding is in `src/lib/subject-media.mjs`, while `docs/media/direct-gis-image-followup-20261015.json` records the precise supersession without altering the 25-entry historical curation baseline.

Image clicks from any listing continue to open the associated content entry. Image links within the content page still lead to the detailed source and licence page. The revised test suite consumes the follow-up register so earlier approved selections remain auditable rather than being overwritten.

## Highest-value unresolved acquisition

The New Zealand Forest Service 1974 original is the strongest further historic replacement if Auckland Libraries provides a usable image matching Map 10213. The MPI map is a stronger example of modern institutional GIS but needs a definitive licence for the exact 2020 image and a version free of restricted emblems. A direct Geographx image should only be published with written clearance or source-specific reuse permission.
