## Scope and rights

The research cutoff is 31 December 2025. No image created after this date is eligible for publication. The three candidate research articles were inspected on the primary MDPI publisher pages on 11 October 2026; each article states that the author material is licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Source figure numbers were compared with the publishers' descriptive figure captions. The individual figure bytes, derivative checksums and successful reuse checks must also pass the acquisition run before publication.

An automated GitHub runner attempting to fetch the article HTML received HTTP 403 on 11 October 2026; the publisher's page was therefore inspected independently using authorised public page access. No anti-bot challenge should be bypassed or simulated. Original figure files may be retrieved only from public publisher-hosted asset URLs; on failure, the candidate remains unpublished. No license permission for separately credited external photographs can be inferred from an article licence.

## Publication criteria

Preserve the accepted 44 chapters and all stable passage identifiers. Validate original download MIME, dimensions and SHA-256, plus uncropped WebP display and card files. Check exact inline paragraph placement, attribution and licence information on the image's own source page, desktop/mobile rendering, link integrity and the full preservation/build tests. Keep the existing contextual images unless the replacement more accurately illustrates its exact subject. Publish only the subset of images for which all gates pass. Record failed or held original URLs without creating misleading picture records.
